Exact Match Prompt Creator

Checking Chrome AI…

Turn a page into prompts you can paste into a chatbot to test whether its retrieval layer can surface your content. Generation runs in your browser. When Chrome on-device AI is available, it helps phrase the questions. Otherwise the app falls back to grounded templates.

Content source

Exact-match mode appends a short verbatim phrase from your page. Disable it for more natural, less-leading questions.

Notes

  • Chrome on-device AI works in supported desktop Chrome builds. Other browsers use templates only.
  • The model downloads once in Chrome; this app does not call a hosted LLM API.
  • A missing chatbot result does not prove your page is absent from its index.
