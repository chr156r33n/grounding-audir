Checking Chrome AI…

Paste page copy and turn it into prompts you can use to test whether a chatbot's retrieval layer can surface your content. Everything stays in your browser. Chrome on-device AI helps phrase the questions when available; otherwise the app uses grounded templates.

Exact-match mode appends a short verbatim phrase from your page. Disable it for more natural, less-leading questions.
